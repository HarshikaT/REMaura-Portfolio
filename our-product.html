Questions, answered

Before you go any deeper.

A few of the questions we hear most often about how REMaura fits into real life.

Most sleep trackers analyze nighttime sleep after it happens. REMaura detects sleep during the day and in real time.

REMaura is designed with privacy-first principles. All data is encrypted and stored securely, and users retain full control of what data is shared. Data is never sold. Any data used for research or algorithm improvement is anonymized and only shared with explicit opt-in consent, in alignment with HIPAA-informed best practices.

Yes! REMaura is specifically designed for daytime use during real world activities. Its interventions are subtle and customizable, allowing users to stay alert without distractions.

If REMaura detects a microsleep, it alerts the user in one of three ways: haptics, cooling, or notifications. They are subtle yet effective, and won't cause disruptions to anyone else. These triggers received the most positive feedback from our survey respondents.

The companion app is designed to turn REMaura's sensing into an understandable picture of your alertness, patterns, logs, and personalized insights.

REMaura is being designed for people who experience excessive daytime sleepiness and want a real-time way to recognize changes in alertness and respond to them.
