Our StoryWhere it began

Our motivation stems from a personal challenge: one of our loved ones has narcolepsy (type I) and is allergic to modafinil, making standard treatments inaccessible. After her diagnosis, she experienced dangerous microsleeps — once falling asleep while driving, and losing her license as a result.

This deeply personal experience inspired us to create a safer, more effective solution for people like her.

Behind the NameREMaura

The name REMaura is inspired by the remora fish, known for its unique symbiotic relationship with other marine animals. By attaching itself to a host, the remora stays closely connected and adapts alongside it — reflecting how REMaura is essentially a companion, closely tuned to the user's physiological signs.

The name also draws from REM sleep, while "aura" represents an invisible layer of protection.
