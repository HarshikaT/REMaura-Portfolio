As a company, we stand for inclusivity, accuracy, and improvement. We put detail and care into even the smallest details — from our logo, to our name, to our device. We hope you'll choose REMaura.

Our StoryWhere it began

Our motivation stems from a personal challenge: Avni's grandmother has narcolepsy (type I) and is allergic to modafinil, making standard treatments inaccessible. After her diagnosis, she experienced dangerous microsleeps — once falling asleep while driving, and losing her license as a result.

This deeply personal experience inspired us to create a safer, more effective solution for people like her.

Behind the NameREMaura

The name REMaura is inspired by the remora fish, known for its unique symbiotic relationship with other marine animals. By attaching itself to a host, the remora stays closely connected and adapts alongside it — reflecting how REMaura is essentially a companion, closely tuned to the user's physiological signs.

The name also draws from REM sleep, while "aura" represents an invisible layer of protection.

Mission, Vision, Values

The heart of our company.

Mission

To protect people from the dangers of microsleeps by continuously monitoring physiological signals and providing intelligent, real-time intervention — empowering individuals with sleep disorders to live safer, more independent lives.

Vision

A future where intelligent, adaptive technology seamlessly protects humans from lapses in alertness — making microsleep-related accidents preventable rather than inevitable.

Values

Guided by safety, human-centered design, and scientific integrity — using ethical, responsible AI that respects privacy while delivering accurate, reliable protection through continuous learning and improvement.
