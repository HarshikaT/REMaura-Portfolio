Henrico County Public Schools

Don't sleep 💤 on these two scholars — their innovative research into sleep science earned them national recognition in the Samsung Solve for Tomorrow competition and a meeting with the CEO of the National Sleep Foundation! 🙌 J.R. Tucker High School sophomore Avni Loya and Deep Run High School sophomore Harshika Thouti were named 2025-2026 Promising STEM Innovation Recipients for developing "REMaura," a wearable accessory and app that is able to detect sleep patterns for people dealing with sleep disorders such as narcolepsy and sleep apnea. Specifically, it can help people with these disorders be aware of "microsleeps" — brief lapses of sleep that can affect people daily or lead to dangerous situations. The students worked closely with sleep health advocates and industry professionals and utilized surveys and data to refine their project with the goal of improving safety and quality of life for people living with sleep disorders. After testing and development, a survey distributed through Project Sleep reported that 94% of respondents said they would purchase REMaura. The two friends each attended Moody Middle School, and now Avni is in the I.B. program at Tucker, while Harshika is in the Center for Information Technology at Deep Run. A group of students from Moody Middle School were also recognized as part of this year's STEM-focused competition. Congratulations to these brilliant students and their hard work!
